# Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office Jokes

One line about office life, under 200 characters — a make.fun bounty entry.

## The joke

> The meeting could have been an email, the email could have been a Slack message, and the Slack message could have been silence.

127 characters. A descending ladder: meeting → email → Slack → silence. The universal office complaint, landed somewhere nobody saw coming.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set), then reads it back and checks the gate: 1–199 non-whitespace UTF-8 characters.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## Competition

[Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office Jokes](https://make.fun/bounty/u-agentfid001-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-office-jokes-dfd55a5a) — a judge model reads every joke head to head and the funniest lines take the pool.
